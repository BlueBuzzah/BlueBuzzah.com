Open vCR Therapy System

BlueBuzzah is an open-source vibrotactile coordinated reset (vCR) therapy device system which delivers gentle, precisely calculated patterns of vibrations to user's fingertips. Based upon peer-reviewed research demonstrating potential symptom relief for Parkinson's patients.

What is BlueBuzzah?

BlueBuzzah is a community-driven, open-source project creating accessible vibrotactile therapy devices for people living with Parkinson's disease. BlueBuzzah is inspired by groundbreaking research on vibrotactile coordinated reset (vCR) stimulation.

The system applies gentle vibrations to fingertips, stimulating sensory neurons connected to motor control regions of the brain. Research suggests this may help address tremor, rigidity, and movement challenges.

Built on readily available hardware and open-source software, BlueBuzzah empowers patients, caregivers, and makers to build their own therapy devices.

How it works

  1. Build your gloves

    Follow the open build guide to put together a pair of gloves. Each fingertip gets a small vibration motor.

  2. Load the firmware

    Plug each glove into your computer. BlueBuzzah Updater loads the firmware and sets which glove is primary and which is secondary.

  3. Start a session

    The gloves pulse each fingertip in a changing order. The BuzzahBuddy phone app, coming soon, will let you start, stop and adjust sessions.

The science behind it

BlueBuzzah is inspired by groundbreaking clinical research on vibrotactile coordinated reset (vCR) stimulation conducted by leading neuroscience researchers.

  • NEURAL REGENERATION RESEARCH · 2021

    Vibrotactile Coordinated Reset Stimulation for the Treatment of Parkinson's Disease

    Tass PA, Stanford University School of Medicine

    A review of vCR research so far. People in early studies reported fewer movement symptoms. Larger studies are still needed.

  • FRONTIERS IN PHYSIOLOGY · 2021

    Coordinated Reset Vibrotactile Stimulation Induces Sustained Cumulative Benefits in Parkinson's Disease

    Pfeifer KJ, Kromer JA, Cook AJ, et al.

    A small study that followed people for 6 months. People reported easier movement over 3 months that lasted to the 6-month check-in. Brain recordings also changed.

The system components

  • Hardware

    In Progress

    Open-source hardware specification utilizing precise LRAs (Linear Resonant Actuators), commodity circuit board components, and 3D-printable enclosures. The battery typically lasts 8 hours or more.

    Build guide

  • Firmware

    The code that operates the gloves. It keeps the primary and secondary gloves in sync and allows for custom control and configuration.

    Firmware docs

  • BlueBuzzah Updater

    Device management Desktop application. Intuitive step-by-step wizards to update and configure your devices over USB on macOS and Windows.

  • BuzzahBuddy

    Early Access

    The smartphone companion app for therapy session control and progress monitoring. Start, stop, and customize your therapy from your phone.

Important

People with implanted electrodes from deep brain stimulation (DBS), heart pacemakers, or other internal electronic devices should take special precautions around all electronic devices, including BlueBuzzah. Consult your physician before use.
