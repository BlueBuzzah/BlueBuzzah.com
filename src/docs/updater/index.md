## Download

<UpdaterDownloads />

On macOS, open the download and drag the Updater into Applications. On Windows, open the download and follow the installer.

## What you'll need

- A USB-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge, and the Updater can't see a glove through them.
- An internet connection the first time you use a firmware version. The Updater downloads firmware from GitHub and keeps a copy on your computer.

## Find your way around

<!-- planned-image: /docs/img/updater/firmware-page | screenshot | the Updater's Firmware page, with the sidebar showing Firmware, Configure and Settings -->

The sidebar has 3 sections. Each one has a keyboard shortcut, shown beside it.

- **Firmware:** install or update the firmware, and set each glove's role. See [Update the firmware](/docs/updater/firmware).
- **Configure:** choose the therapy pattern and how the light behaves. See [Configure the gloves](/docs/updater/configure).
- **Settings:** the theme, high contrast, and the Updater's version.

The Updater calls each glove a device. While a flash or setup runs, the other sections stay locked until it finishes.

## Updates to the Updater

The Updater keeps itself up to date. When you open it, it checks for a new version of itself and shows **A New BlueBuzzah Updater Is Ready** if there is one. Select **Install and Restart**, or **Not Now** to keep working. It never asks during a flash or setup.

To check yourself, open **Settings** and select **Check for Updates**.
