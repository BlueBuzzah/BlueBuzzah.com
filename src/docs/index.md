## What you're building

A pair means two of everything: two boards, eight tactors, two cases and two batteries. There's no soldering anywhere in this build.

Each glove has four tactors, one per finger: index, middle, ring and little. A tactor is the part that buzzes against your fingertip. The board, battery and antenna sit in a small case on the back of the hand.

BlueBuzzah is based on Dr. Peter Tass's research on vibrotactile Coordinated Reset (vCR). Read [the vCR research paper](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8055937/) for the background.

Read [Safety and disclaimers](/docs/safety) before you order parts or start a build.

## What it costs

A pair costs roughly $165–545, excluding tools you own. Prices change with supplier, quantity and shipping. [Get the parts](/docs/parts#cost) breaks the cost down.
